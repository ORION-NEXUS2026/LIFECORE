# Code of Conduct

We want Lifecore to be a friendly, useful project for students, makers and researchers.

- Be respectful and patient. Welcome newcomers and questions of any level.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Do not share other people's private data (WiFi passwords, personal recordings, health data).
- Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning when you disagree.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and block people, when these rules are broken.
To report a problem, open a private security/conduct report from the repository's **Security** tab or contact a maintainer directly.
This text is based on the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
